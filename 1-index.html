🍲 Cooker Whistle Counter

Counts pressure-cooker whistles and alerts you at your target - so rice and dal don't overcook while you're nearby doing something else.

A cooking aid, not a safety device. Microphone detection can miss whistles or count background noise (TV, pressure horns, mixers). Stay within earshot of a cooker in use, and never leave a pressure cooker unattended relying on this page. When in doubt, use manual tap mode.

Honest limits
  • Works only while this tab is open, the screen stays on, and mic permission is granted.
  • Background noise (pressure horns, TV, mixi, another cooker) can add false counts; loud kitchens can mask real whistles. Check the count against your ears.
  • Whistle loudness varies by cooker and stove. Do a quick test whistle near the mic before relying on auto mode for that session.
  • On phones, some browsers pause the mic when the screen locks - manual mode still works for counting after.
Built after a Reddit request: "an app to count the number of whistles a cooker has completed while cooking" - source thread. Everything runs in your browser; no audio or data leaves your device.
